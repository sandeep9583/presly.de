How quoting works

From first email to a signed quote in a week.

We do not run a qualification gauntlet. Tell us your shape, get a number, and decide. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

Prefer email? Send your requirement to contact@presly.de and we will reply with questions or a quotation.
1

You send the shape of your business

Projects, approximate plot count, number of users, how commission works today and where your data currently lives.

A 30-minute call, or a short email thread if you prefer. We check the details that actually change the number.

Package, modules, user count, implementation scope, timeline and any configuration work itemised separately. Valid for 30 days.

4

Decision, then onboarding

Commercial terms

The parts people ask about before signing

Annual subscription

Presly is licensed annually rather than monthly, because implementation is a real project and month-to-month pricing encourages bad rollouts. Longer commitments are priced accordingly.

Implementation fee

Data import, configuration, commission rule setup and training are quoted as a one-time fee. It is itemised in the proposal so you can see exactly what you are buying.

Users included

Each package includes a user allowance. Extra accounts are added at a per-user rate — no charge for read-only management viewers.

Growth without renegotiation

Add a venture, a block or a sales team mid-term and the subscription is adjusted pro rata. You are not forced to wait for a renewal.

No lock-in

Your data exports at any time in structured formats, and the export functions are included in every package. There is no exit fee and no withheld dataset.

Custom development

If a requirement genuinely needs new development, it is scoped and priced as a separate piece of work with its own acceptance criteria — never bundled vaguely into the subscription.
