About Presly

We build the software that runs the business, not the website that advertises it.

Presly is a software company. Our current product, Presly Estate, is an ERP for real-estate developers, builders and brokerages — the operational core where inventory, sales, bookings and commission live.

Why real estate first

The real-estate industry runs on some of the most consequential operational data there is. Which plots exist, what they cost, who was promised what, which instalments are due and what the sales team has earned. In a great many companies, all of it lives in a handful of spreadsheets that four people edit and nobody fully trusts.

That gap is not a marketing problem — it is a software problem, and it is a serious one. Selling the same plot twice, paying the wrong commission, or discovering at the end of a quarter that nobody knows how many units are actually unsold are failures that cost real money and damage real trust.

So we started where the consequences are highest and the tooling is weakest. Presly Estate models land and buildings the way the business actually thinks about them, and enforces the rules that stop those failures from happening.

What we believe

Software should enforce, not just record.

If a system lets two people book the same plot, it has not solved the problem — it has digitised it. Constraint is a feature.

Operators know their process better than we do.

We configure stages, hierarchies, approval limits and commission rules to match you. We do not arrive with a process and ask you to adopt it.

Honesty is a sales strategy.

If we are not the right fit, we say so in the first conversation. A bad-fit customer is worse for us than a lost deal.

No lock-in, ever.

Your data exports in full at any time, included in every package. Software should be kept by being good, not by being inescapable.

How we work

A small team, close to the product.

We are deliberately not a large sales organisation with a thin engineering layer behind it. The people who scope your rollout are the people who can change the software.

Direct access to builders

Requirements go from a customer conversation to the people writing the code, without passing through three layers of account management first.

Scoped, itemised proposals

Fixed pricing with configuration and custom work called out separately. Nobody should have to discover the real cost after signing.

Ship continuously

Improvements reach all customers on the managed cloud without a migration project. Your feedback tends to show up in the product rather than a backlog.

Careful with your data

Company-level isolation at the database layer, encryption, backups and an audit trail. We treat your buyer data as something we are responsible for.

We document what we build

Architecture and module documentation maintained alongside the code, so your team and ours both understand how the system behaves.

We stay after go-live

The risky moment is your first booking cycle and first payout run. We are close through both, then move to a normal support rhythm.

What is next

Real estate is where we are. Not where we stop.

Presly is a software company rather than a single-product shop. The foundation we have built — inventory that cannot be double-sold, a sales pipeline tied to physical assets, configurable approval hierarchies and a commission engine — applies to any business that sells finite, individually identified things through a commissioned sales force.

We are not going to promise dates for products that do not exist yet. What we will say plainly is that real estate is our focus now, and the platform underneath it is deliberately general.
