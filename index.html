What we do

We build software for companies that sell land and buildings.

Most real-estate businesses run on four or five disconnected tools: a spreadsheet for inventory, a CRM nobody updates, WhatsApp for approvals and Tally for money. Presly replaces that with one system that knows which plots exist, who is selling them and what is owed to whom.

How Presly Estate works

Inventory you can trust

Plots are records, not rows in a file. Availability, holds, bookings and registration are tracked against the plot itself — so two salespeople can never sell the same one.

Sales that move faster

Leads, site visits, negotiations and bookings sit in one pipeline. Every call, visit and price revision is on the record, next to the plot it concerns.

Commission without arguments

Define your slabs and splits once. The engine calculates what each agent and channel partner earned, per booking, with the working shown.

Plots customers understand

Show a 3D view of the layout, the road frontage, the corner premium and the price — the same view your team sees while standing on site.

The platform

Seven things Presly Estate does properly

Not a feature list for its own sake — these are the processes that decide whether a real-estate business has a good month or a chaotic one.

Plot & inventory management

Projects → ventures → phases → blocks → plots. Dimensions, facing, corner and road-facing premiums, rate per square metre and price history on every single plot.

  • Live availability matrix by block
  • Holds with automatic expiry
  • Bulk price revision by block or phase

Lead & customer CRM

Capture leads from walk-ins, ads, exhibitions and channel partners. Assign them round-robin, track every follow-up, and convert a lead into a customer without duplicating a single record.

  • Configurable pipeline stages
  • Duplicate detection on phone number
  • Full activity timeline per contact

Booking & agreement workflow

Holds, bookings, co-applicants, payment schedules and agreement status. Approvals follow the hierarchy you already use, not a workflow we invented.

  • Locked booking transaction — no double sales
  • Co-applicant and joint-ownership support
  • Cancellation and refund handling

Commission engine

Slabs, percentages, flat fees, launch incentives and channel-partner splits. Run a payout cycle and get a per-agent statement you can hand to a finance team.

  • Rule-based, per booking
  • Agent and agency split in one run
  • Exportable payout schedule

3D plot visualisation

Turn the plot matrix into a spatial layout your buyers can read at a glance: roads, corners, orientation and the plot they are being offered, highlighted.

  • Interactive layout by block
  • Colour-coded availability
  • Shareable view for site offices

Reports management reads

Booking velocity by venture, conversion by lead source, ageing inventory, collections due and agent productivity — the numbers a director asks for on a Monday morning.

  • Role-based dashboards
  • Scheduled email reports
  • Excel and CSV export everywhere

Teams, roles & hierarchy

Regional heads, project managers, sales managers and agents each see their own book of business. Permissions follow the reporting line you already have.

  • Team-scoped data access
  • Approval limits by seniority
  • Audit trail on every change

Integrations & data

Accounting, payments, messaging and your existing website forms. Presly sits in the middle and keeps everyone's numbers the same.

  • REST API for everything in the UI
  • Webhooks on booking and payment events
  • Import from your current spreadsheets

Beyond real estate

Presly is a software company, not a single-product shop. The same platform foundation — inventory, sales, commission, approvals — is what we will apply to the next industries we enter.

3D plot visualisation

A layout your buyer understands in five seconds.

Numbers on a price list do not sell a plot. Orientation, road frontage, what is next door and how the light falls do. Presly renders your survey layout as an interactive 3D view, coloured by live availability.

  • Select any plot to see dimensions, facing and all-in price in place
  • Corner and road-facing premiums shown as they are calculated
  • Identical view in the sales office and on a tablet at the site
  • Updates the moment a plot is held or booked
Commission

Commission calculated, not argued about.

In most companies, commission is a spreadsheet that three people maintain and nobody trusts. Presly computes it from the booking itself, applies the rules you configured, and keeps the arithmetic visible so anyone can check it.

  • Rules, not formulas. Percentage, slab, flat fee, tiered bonus, launch incentive.
  • Every party in one run. In-house agents and external channel partners, split correctly.
  • Survives revisions. If a booking value changes, the commission is recomputed, not manually patched.
  • Audit-ready. Every payout line traces back to a booking, a rule and a date.

Sales

From first enquiry to registered sale, one thread.

A lead arrives from an ad, a walk-in or a channel partner. It gets assigned, followed up, taken to a site visit, negotiated on a specific plot and converted into a booking. Presly keeps that entire thread attached to the same person and the same plot.

  • Sensible round-robin so leads do not rot in one inbox
  • Site visits logged against the plots actually shown
  • Source-wise conversion, so you know which ads pay for themselves
  • Nothing lost when a salesperson leaves — the history stays

Trust & deployment

Your data, your rules on where it lives.

Real-estate companies hold customer identity documents, pricing and contract terms. We treat that as something to be careful with, not as marketing copy.

Complete data separation

Every record is scoped to your company at the database level, not just filtered in the interface. One customer's data can never appear in another's reports.

Granular permissions

Decide who can see a rate list, who can approve a discount, who can cancel a booking and who can release a payout run.

Cloud or your own servers

Run Presly Estate as a managed cloud service, or deploy it inside your own infrastructure if your policies require it.

Backups and continuity

Scheduled backups, point-in-time recovery and a documented restore procedure — tested, not assumed.
